# ADR-0006: Use Stylized, Policy-compliant Destruction

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The experience must remain appropriate and publishable for a child account.

## Decision

Use original characters and clearly fictional interiors such as foam, candy, crystals, glow, or mechanical components. Exclude realistic gore and protected media.

## Reason

The constraint supports the intended audience and target Minimal or Mild content classification.

## Consequences

Art, names, particles, sounds, thumbnails, and public branding require a policy-minded review before publishing.

## Alternatives rejected

Realistic anatomy, graphic wounds, copied Brainrot characters, and reliance on an adult production account are incompatible with the project goal.
